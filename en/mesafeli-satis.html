ARTICLE 5 — FREE TRIAL PERIOD

The BUYER may try the Producer plan free of charge for 14 (fourteen) days. A valid payment method must be provided to start the trial; no charge is made during the trial period.

Automatic conversion to a paid subscription: at the end of the trial, unless cancelled beforehand by the BUYER, the subscription automatically converts to the paid Producer plan and the current Producer price stated in ARTICLE 3 (or the yearly amount if yearly billing was selected) is charged to the payment method on file. If cancelled before the trial ends, no charge is made.

Cancelling the trial: the trial may be cancelled at any time before it ends; if it is cancelled before it ends, no charge is made.

The ways to cancel are set out in ARTICLE 8.

ARTICLE 6 — DELIVERY

The service subject to sale is digital in nature and is defined to the BUYER's account immediately upon confirmation of payment. There is no physical delivery.

ARTICLE 7 — RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL

Pursuant to Article 49/4 of Law No. 6502 and Article 15/ğ of the Regulation on Distance Contracts, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for contracts concerning services performed instantly in electronic form and intangible goods delivered instantly to the consumer, once performance has begun with the consumer's consent.

By completing the subscription purchase, the BUYER is deemed to have accepted this.

Nevertheless, in cases such as the service being completely unusable or a technical malfunction, a refund may be considered at the SELLER's discretion. For requests: info@voxomix.com

ARTICLE 8 — SUBSCRIPTION RENEWAL AND CANCELLATION

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of the period. The BUYER may cancel at any time, from a single place:

On cancellation the plan stays active until the end of the current paid period; no charge is made for the following period. No partial mid-period refund is made. For the free trial, see ARTICLE 5.
