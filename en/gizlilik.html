Privacy Policy

How we process your personal data

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This English text is an unofficial translation provided for convenience. The Turkish version of this policy is the legally binding one; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Data Controller

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed within the scope of the VoxoMix application and the www.voxomix.com website, operated by Barış Demirel, under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

2. Data Collected

The following data may be collected while you use our service:

  • Account information: Email address, first and last name (during sign-up)
  • Payment information: Subscription plan and payment date (card details are processed by the payment provider — iyzico in Türkiye, Polar outside Türkiye — and are never seen or stored by us)
  • Usage data: Number of songs uploaded, features used
  • Technical data: Operating system, app version

3. Purpose of Data Use

The data collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining the service
  • Subscription management and payment processing
  • Providing technical support
  • Improving the service
  • Fulfilling legal obligations
  • Where you have given explicit consent; sending commercial electronic messages by email, such as new-version notices, price updates, campaigns and new-product announcements (see Section 10)

4. Local Data Processing

The VoxoMix desktop application processes the music files you upload only on your local computer. Your audio files are never sent to or stored on any external server.

6. Data Security

Industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and security measures are applied to protect your data. Your payment information is processed on PCI-DSS compliant payment infrastructure: iyzico for purchases made from Türkiye, Polar for purchases made from outside Türkiye.

7. Data Retention Period

Your account data is retained while your account is active and for 2 years after the account is deleted. Payment records are kept for 10 years as required by law.

8. Your Rights Under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • To request information if it has been processed
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose
  • To know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom the data is transferred
  • To request correction if the data is incomplete or inaccurate
  • To request deletion or destruction of the data within the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK
  • To request that correction, deletion and destruction requests be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred
  • To object to an adverse outcome produced solely by automated analysis of the processed data
  • To claim compensation if you suffer loss because your data was processed unlawfully

To exercise these rights, you may contact info@voxomix.com. Your request is answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

9. Cookies and Site Measurement

Our website stores a small amount of information in your browser that is required for it to work (your language preference and the cookie choice described below). These are not used for advertising or tracking.

Beyond that, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. This measurement runs only with your explicit consent: unless you select “Accept” in the banner shown on your first visit, no analytics cookie is set and no request is sent to Google.

If you consent, the data processed is: the pages you visit, the source you arrived from, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser information, and interactions such as download and button clicks. This data is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) and may be stored on servers outside your country. Your name, email address and account details are not included in this measurement.

An important distinction: this measurement applies to the voxomix.com website only. The VoxoMix desktop app does not send your music files to any server for analysis; stem separation, chord, tempo and lyrics processing all happen on your computer.

Server-side download counter: to measure how often the download link is clicked, our server stores only a timestamp — no IP address, country, device or browser information is recorded. Because this count cannot be linked to any person, it runs independently of cookie consent.

10. Commercial Electronic Messages and Marketing

With the separate, explicit opt-in consent you provide during sign-up, we may send you commercial electronic messages by email, such as new-version notices, price updates, campaigns and new-product announcements. This consent is not a precondition for registering for the service; you can fully use the service even if you do not check it.

Commercial electronic messages are sent within the scope of Law No. 6563 on the Regulation of Electronic Commerce and its related regulation. Your consents are recorded in the Message Management System (İYS).

Opt-out: You may exercise your right to refuse free of charge at any time via the link at the bottom of every commercial email, by sending a request to info@voxomix.com, or through İYS. Your refusal request is processed within 3 business days at the latest.

Note: Informational emails relating to the operation of the service — such as subscription renewal, payment confirmation, email verification and password reset — are not commercial electronic messages and are therefore sent independently of this consent.

11. Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the rights set out in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in addition to the KVKK rights above. The data controller is Barış Demirel; contact: info@voxomix.com.

Where data is processed: your data is processed in Türkiye and on the servers of the providers listed in Section 5 (including the EU, the United Kingdom and the United States). Where those providers transfer data outside the EEA, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent safeguards apply.

Automated decision-making: we do not carry out solely automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you.

Retention: the periods stated in Section 7 apply.

Children: the service is not directed at persons under 13. If the digital age of consent in your country is higher (it may be up to 16 under GDPR Art. 8), parental or guardian consent is required before creating an account.
